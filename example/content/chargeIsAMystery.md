# Charge is a mystery

Electric charge is one of the most fundamental properties of matter — and nobody truly knows **why** it exists.

We know charges come in two types: positive and negative. We know like charges repel and opposite charges attract. We know the electron carries a charge of exactly $-1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ coulombs.

But *why does charge exist at all?* Why are there exactly two types? Why is the electron's charge equal and opposite to the proton's, even though they are completely different particles?

These questions remain unanswered. Charge is simply a primitive fact about the universe.

## What we do know

Charge is:
- **Quantized** — it comes in discrete multiples of a base unit
- **Conserved** — the total charge of an isolated system never changes
- **Lorentz invariant** — charge doesn't change when you move fast
