## The Standard Model & Matter-Antimatter

The Standard Model of particle physics is the theory classifying all known elementary particles. It describes three of the four known fundamental forces (electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions), while leaving out gravity. 

### Matter and Antimatter

Every type of fundamental particle in the Standard Model has a corresponding antimatter particle. These antiparticles have the same mass but opposite charge and other quantum numbers. For example, the antiparticle of the electron is the positron.

### Excitations

When a quantum field receives enough energy, it forms an excitation, which manifests as a particle. If a matter field and an antimatter field interact, they can annihilate each other, releasing their energy back into the electromagnetic field as photons. Conversely, a high-energy photon can spontaneously create a matter-antimatter particle pair.
